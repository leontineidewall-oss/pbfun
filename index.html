How it works

The record is the wallet.

pump.fun already pays a creator fee on every trade. pb.fun points that fee at the one person every speedrun community already agrees on: the world record holder.

1

Post your run

Reply to a run on X, tag @usepb, add the game, category and a $TICKER.

2

A coin launches

It goes live on pump.fun with its own pool wallet as the creator, so the creator fee fills the pool.

3

The record holder earns

The pool pays whoever holds the verified world record on speedrun.com, in SOL.

4

Beat it, take it

When a faster run is verified, fees from then on go to the new holder. What was paid stays paid.

    FAQ

    Questions.

    Do record holders have to do anything?

    No. Fees wait for them. To claim, they sign in with the speedrun.com account that holds the record and connect a wallet. Unclaimed fees never expire and nobody else can claim them.

    What if a run gets rejected or removed?

    pb.fun follows the verified leaderboard. If the top run changes for any reason, fees from that moment on go to whoever is now on top. Nothing already paid is taken back.

    Which categories count?

    Whatever speedrun.com lists as a full-game category. Levels and subcategories are planned later. The lookup above shows the category's default leaderboard.

    Is this live?

    No. No coins have launched through pb.fun, and there is no contract address. Anything claiming otherwise is not us.

    What can go wrong?

    Memecoins are extremely volatile and most go to zero, so fees can be zero. speedrun.com could change or limit its API. pump.fun could change its fees or program. None of this is financial advice.
